# Provenance

This repository is a clean-room reconstruction created in September 2026 from:

- A recovered University of Guelph ENGG3050 Lab 1 handout, Fall 2024 edition.
- A recovered Vivado waveform screenshot from Aymen's work.
- A recovered screenshot of a synthesis integration error.
- Aymen's September 2026 ChatGPT conversation about implementing and debugging the lab.

The original VHDL source, XDC file, Vivado project, report and bitstream were not recovered. All source files in this public draft were newly written for the reconstruction. They must not be described as the untouched original submission.

The course handout and private recovery images are intentionally excluded from this public directory. The reconstructed self-checking adder testbench passed all 8,192 combinations on NVC 1.23.0 on September 22, 2026. Synthesis and hardware validation on a Nexys A7-100T remain outstanding.
